Close all four. Every single day.

Four habits, one circle. Each habit is a quarter of the Seal: steps, calories, weigh-in and bath. Three of them fill themselves from Health Connect; the bath is one long press. When all four quarters close, the circle lights up in all four colours, the day becomes a Perfect Day, and you rip open a sticker pack. Every new sticker is a new animated cat breed for your album.

Below: a playable cover screen, the full set of cover and inner screen states across one day, the psychology behind each one, and four decisions I need from you. Nothing here is built yet. Approve or send changes, then I build it.

Before I build

Four calls for you

If you just say "approved", I'll go with the recommendation on each.

1. Sticker swaps or hardcore?

Swaps: duplicate stickers become swaps, and trading 3 saves a missed day. Hardcore: no saves, one miss and the streak is back to zero.

Recommend swaps. Duplicates stay exciting, and one bad dinner can't wipe out 40 days.

2. When does the calorie quarter lock?

You tap Close kitchen whenever you're done eating; if you forget, it locks automatically at a set time.

Recommend auto-lock at 8:30 PM, changeable in settings. Late logs after lock still count against you.

3. How pushy are notifications?

Morning pledge, midday nudge, 9 PM "at risk" and 11 PM last call, only for habits still open. Or a quieter two.

Recommend all four, each skipped automatically once the day is closed.

4. Sounds and haptics?

A soft click as each quarter closes and a richer chime plus haptic roll on a Perfect Day.

Recommend haptics always, sound on (follows your ringer mode).
